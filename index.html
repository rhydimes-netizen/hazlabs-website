About us

Technical consulting, design, implementation and maintenance → one team, from licence to pollution control system.

We specialise in facilitating regulatory compliance and obtaining licences from the Tamil Nadu Pollution Control Board (TNPCB) and the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB). Our work runs in three lines.
